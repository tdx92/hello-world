## The core idea

> Blackfox Studio creates tactile objects from natural materials, using fire, texture and restrained design to bring a sense of nature and quiet character into modern homes.

The simplest version, which the owner wants protected:

> Natural timber. Shaped by fire. Designed with restraint. Made to be felt.

It is not "burnt wood furniture". It is furniture people want to look at, touch and live with. The surface itself is the experience. Most furniture says "look at me"; Blackfox says "touch me".

**Lines the owner uses:**
- Feel the grain. (Possibly the strongest.)
- Made to be felt.
- Shaped by fire.
- A piece of nature in your house.
- Current site tagline: *Shaped by fire. Inspired by nature. Made to be felt.*

**The owner's own warning:** don't let Blackfox get complicated. It does not need ten philosophies, twenty product categories and fifty slogans.

## Philosophy

- **Fire.** A design tool, not a black finish. Burning changes the timber itself: it reveals grain, creates depth, texture and irregularity, and produces natural tonal variation, so every piece is slightly different.
- **Wabi-Sabi.** Imperfection, asymmetry, natural materials, simplicity, texture, age, light and shadow, things becoming more beautiful through use. The imperfection is intentional. Not "look how perfect my woodworking is" but "the character of the timber is part of the design".
- **Shibui.** Simplicity without sterility. Restrained forms, natural materials, tactile surfaces, quiet visual impact, asymmetry where it fits, beauty that does not need to shout. Quiet and intentional, never generic rustic.
- **Italian identity.** Italian design sensibility plus Japanese restraint, natural timber, fire and Marco's own craft. Blackfox is **not** "Japanese furniture made in Auckland". It is Marco's interpretation of natural, tactile furniture, influenced by Italian design and Japanese philosophies.

## The finish system

Three named finishes, used as a consistent Blackfox finish language:

| Finish | Look |
|---|---|
| **Hikari** | Light, warm, heavily textured |
| **Gendai** | Medium warm brown, textured |
| **Yakisugi** | Very dark charcoal to black, heavily charred, deep crocodile-like texture |

They also combine, for example a **Yakisugi base with a Gendai top**, so not every piece is all black. Blackfox is not a "Yakisugi studio": fire is one part of a broader design language, and the range can grow beyond black.

## How to talk about custom work

Everything can be made to order, but never frame it as "tell me what you want and I'll build anything". That reads as a handyman service and undermines the premium position. The framing the owner wants:

> Blackfox creates a defined design language, then adapts it to your space.

## Aquarium cabinets

Marco has genuine aquascaping knowledge: planted tanks, layouts, lighting, cabinets, equipment, and how furniture works around an aquarium. That is what makes the cabinets credible rather than a trend. A future "Blackfox Aquascape" subcategory is an idea, not a launched line. A collaboration with an aquarium shop has only been considered, so never claim a partnership.

## How it's made

Safe to describe on the site:

- Rough-sawn, knotty-grade pine, chosen deliberately so the knots, grain and irregularity stay part of the character.
- Burned by hand with an LPG torch, aiming for deep char and a crocodile-like texture.
- Brushed back, shaped and joined by hand in the studio.
- Sealed with a water-based protective finish so it stands up to dents, scratches, water marks and daily use, without killing the texture. Never a glossy, plastic-coated surface. The texture has to stay alive.

The material is inexpensive pine, but it must never read as "I grabbed some cheap timber". It is **timber selected for its character, with fire used to reveal it**.

## The fox

The fox stands for calm, peace, quietness, imperfect elegance, observation and nature, which is why the name fits the brand's quieter philosophy. The logo is a simplified, symmetrical fox: two ears, a tail, a simple silhouette. It is laser-engraved and also used as a stencil for bleach designs on black Yakisugi-style T-shirts.

**The logo does not change.** Use the existing logo files.

## Who it's for

Local Auckland people interested in interiors, architecture, plants, natural materials, design, aquariums, craftsmanship, slow living, distinctive furniture and tactile materials, and willing to pay more than Marketplace prices. The reaction to aim for: *"That would look incredible in my house."*

Blackfox should create **brand comparison** ("this is a Blackfox piece"), not price comparison ("$700 table versus $500 table").

## Look and feel

**Must feel:** elegant, premium, expensive, warm, tactile, natural, imperfect, real. A design brand, not a Marketplace woodworker. A premium design studio, not a basic local furniture listing.

**Must not look like:** cheap rustic furniture, generic DIY furniture, overly farmhouse styling, ring boxes, Japandi clichés, mass-produced pieces.

**Imagery:** close to high-end architectural and interior photography. Dark backgrounds, natural light, shadows, premium environments. The goal is for the product to look expensive before anyone reads the caption.

**Premium means:** excellent photography, a restrained logo, quality finishes, considered design, strong presentation, consistent product names, consistent finishes, professional communication, and no bargain-bin visual language. The product stays handmade and imperfect while the presentation is refined.

**Owner's requirement:** a black background.

For continuity, the current site uses: background `#0D0C0B`, cards `#1A1714`, gold accent `#FBBF12`, tan `#CA9352`, brown `#775732`, light text `#F5F4F3`, typeface Rethink Sans. These are the current choices, not fixed rules.
